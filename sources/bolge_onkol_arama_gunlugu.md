# Önkol (dirsek ve önkol) – arama günlüğü

Date: 2026-10-31. Discovery used the server-side WebSearch tool. Every candidate was checked against the AWS PMC OA bucket with `scripts/pmc_oa_fetch.py info`, which reads `license_code` from the bucket JSON and figure captions from the JATS XML. Selected figures were downloaded with `pmc_oa_fetch.py get` into `topografik/images/raw/PMC<id>/`, and every downloaded image was opened and checked by eye. `sources/bolge_onkol.json` is generated by `scripts/build_bolge_onkol.py`, which copies captions, citations and licences verbatim from the downloaded files. `scripts/pmc_xml_grep.py` was used to pull quoted numbers from the article text. The total downloaded for this region is about 8.7 MB.
